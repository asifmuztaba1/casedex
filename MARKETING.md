> Last updated: 2026-04-13

This document is the operational marketing playbook for CaseDex. It covers positioning, audience, channels, content, growth loops, and a week-by-week action plan — from zero to a repeatable acquisition engine.

---

## 1. Where We Are Today

- **Product:** CaseDex — legal case management SaaS for Bangladesh (cases, hearings, documents, cause-list auto-sync, AI summaries, offline/PWA, Bangla UI, bKash/Nagad payments).

- **Goal for next 90 days:** Get the first **50 paying chambers / 200 active lawyers**, validate willingness-to-pay, collect 10+ written testimonials, and establish a content engine that produces weekly output.

## 3. Target Audience (ICP)

### Primary: Small Chambers (2–10 lawyers)
- **Why:** Feel the pain of coordination, have budget, decision cycle short
- **Where they hang out:** Bar association groups on FB, WhatsApp chambers groups, court premises
- **Price sensitivity:** Medium — will pay BDT 500–2,000/lawyer/month if ROI clear

### Secondary: Solo Lawyers (Junior / mid-career)
- **Why:** Largest volume, highest acquisition potential, word-of-mouth multiplier
- **Where:** Facebook, WhatsApp, YouTube, bar library, mofussil courts
- **Price sensitivity:** High — need strong free tier + affordable Solo plan (৳500/mo)

### Tertiary: Mid-size firms (10–30 lawyers)
- **Why:** Higher ACV, but longer sales cycle and more objections (data security, IT buy-in)
- **Approach:** Come back to these after 3–6 months of social proof

### Anti-persona (do NOT target early)
- Large firms (50+ lawyers) — they have bespoke tools and 6-month procurement
- Corporate in-house counsel — different workflow, not cause-list driven
- Judges / court staff — not customers

## 4. The Funnel

```
AWARENESS  →  INTEREST  →  SIGN-UP  →  ACTIVATION  →  PAID  →  ADVOCACY
  (FB/WA)    (website)    (/register)  (first case)  (LS)   (referral)
```

| Stage | Goal | Key metric | Tactics |
|-------|------|------------|---------|
| Awareness | Lawyer hears the name | Reach, impressions | FB page, organic posts, WhatsApp sharing |
| Interest | They visit the site | Click-through rate | Pricing page, features page, demo video |
| Sign-up | Free account created | Sign-ups/week | CTA buttons, "free to start" messaging |
| Activation | Creates 1st case, adds 1 hearing | % activated in 7 days | Onboarding emails, in-app nudges |
| Paid | Upgrades to Solo/Practice/Chambers | Trial-to-paid conversion | Trial-ending emails, feature gating |
| Advocacy | Refers a colleague | Referrals per user | Referral program, testimonial collection |

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Lawyers (WAL) — someone who opened the app and viewed/updated at least one case in the last 7 days.

## 5. Channels (Priority-Ordered)

### Tier 1 — Start here (Week 1–4)

#### 5.1 Facebook Page + Groups
**Why:** BD lawyers live on Facebook. Highest leverage channel.

**Action items:**
- Post 3x/week on the CaseDex page (content plan in §7)
- Join these group categories and engage before posting:
  - "Lawyers Club Bangladesh"
  - "Bangladesh Supreme Court Bar Association" groups
  - District bar association groups (Dhaka, Chittagong, Sylhet, Khulna, Rajshahi)
  - Judicial service aspirant groups (future-lawyer awareness)
- **Rule:** Comment on 5 posts before you post once. Build reputation first.
- Pin a video demo to the page

#### 5.2 WhatsApp — Personal outreach
**Why:** Highest conversion. Warm network is gold at this stage.

**Action items:**
- List 20 lawyer friends/acquaintances — personal 1-on-1 messages (scripts in §8)
- Ask each converted user: "পরিচিত আর কোনো উকিল ভাই আছে যাকে আমি পাঠাতে পারি?"
- Create a "CaseDex Early Users" WhatsApp group for beta feedback + community

#### 5.3 YouTube — Demo content
**Why:** Lawyers search YouTube for tutorials. Low competition in BD legal-tech niche.

**Action items:**
- Record 5 videos (2–5 min each):
  1. "CaseDex এ প্রথম মামলা কিভাবে যোগ করবেন" (how-to)
  2. "কজলিস্ট auto-check — যেভাবে কোনো শুনানি মিস হয় না" (killer feature)
  3. "AI দিয়ে case summary" (wow factor)
  4. "টিম মেম্বার অ্যাড করা ও শেয়ার করা" (for chambers)
  5. "বিকাশ/নগদে কিভাবে পেমেন্ট করবেন" (unblock objection)

## 6. Growth Loops Baked Into the Product

These are retention/virality features — not marketing messages, but they *produce* marketing:

### 6.1 Dopamine loop (drives retention)
Build in this priority order:

| # | Feature | Loop it creates |
|---|---------|-----------------|
| 1 | **Daily briefing push** ("আজ ২টি শুনানি, ১টি ডেডলাইন") | Morning habit loop |
| 2 | **Win tracker + celebration** | Updates outcomes → proud → returns |
| 3 | **Case progress bar** | Incomplete → obsession to complete |
| 4 | **Client status update share** (WhatsApp) | Lawyer looks professional → client sees CaseDex branding → referral |
| 5 | **Streak tracker** | Don't break the streak |
| 6 | **Weekly recap email** | Passive reminder of value |

### 6.2 Client-facing exposure loop
When a lawyer shares a status update with their client via WhatsApp:
- Message includes "Sent via CaseDex" footer (optional, toggle-able for Practice+)
- Client sees CaseDex → if the client has their own lawyer → potential new sign-up
- Free top-of-funnel distribution

### 6.3 Referral program
- **Refer a lawyer, both get 1 month free**
- Unique referral link in the app
- Leaderboard of top referrers (soft gamification)
- Start this AFTER you have 20+ happy paying users, so referrers have something to say

### 6.4 Testimonial loop
- After a user hits 30 days active + has done ≥10 cases: prompt for a testimonial
- Offer: 1 free month of upgrade for a 30-second video testimonial
- Use testimonials in FB ads, landing page, WhatsApp outreach

## 12. Risks & Mitigation

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|------------|------------|
| Lawyers don't trust cloud / data security concerns | High | Security page, tenant isolation messaging, local hosting badge |
| Cause-list scraper breaks (upstream portal changes) | Medium | Alert monitoring + dedicated fix SLA; messaging falls back to manual entry |
| Free tier too generous → no upgrades | Medium | Gate AI credits, team size, and storage — measure upgrade rate weekly |
| Competitor copies the cause-list integration | Medium | Build moats: AI summaries, Bangla depth, WhatsApp flows, relationships with bar |
| Founder bandwidth (you are solo) | High | Automate content scheduling, use templates, hire part-time writer from Month 2 |
